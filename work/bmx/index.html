Creating spaces for health and connection

As a Men’s Health and Wellness Advocate with Black Men’s Xchange, I supported culturally grounded wellness programming and community engagement for Black men and same-gender-loving (SGL) communities. My work brought together facilitation, outreach, event planning, partnerships, and digital communications.

I approached wellness as more than a single event: the work was about making room for connection, reflection, practical support, and community leadership.

Programs & gatherings

My work included support for a range of spaces and experiences, including:

Men’s circles & conversation

SGL Men’s Circle, VIBE Check, and King’s Table.

Community gathering

Black Men’s Soul Brunch and Orange Album Theory.

Wellness outdoors

Wellness Wonder Walk and Field Frolic.

Youth partnership

Breaking Bread: Breaking Cycles, connected to a Morgan State University collaboration.

Inside the SGL Men’s Circle

I facilitated circle conversations that created space for Black men and same-gender-loving (SGL) participants to connect, reflect, and share community knowledge. The work centered trust, belonging, and culturally grounded wellness.

Participant privacy: This public case study describes the program without including participant names, photographs, or recordings.

Partnerships & resource development

I contributed to partnership development connected to a $7,500 Allstate Foundation grant and helped advance a Morgan State University collaboration for a nine-month, $50,000 youth wellness pilot. These efforts brought community wellness work into conversation with funders and institutional partners.

Across the role, I facilitated activities, supported outreach and event workflows, and helped partners move from shared interest toward concrete plans.

What guided my practice

Listen first, honor lived experience, and create welcoming spaces where Black men and SGL community members can build trust and connection.
